# Jarvis Finance — Workspace V1

The main workspace is now a market terminal. Paper trading remains available as a dedicated Practice Lab rather than being displayed on the primary terminal.

## Workspace views

- Terminal: live market view, chart, top-of-book, instrument search and account status.
- Markets: market discovery/radar surface.
- Portfolio: connected-account and balance surface; no synthetic balances.
- Automation: automation/runtime status surface.
- Research: strategy and AI research surface.
- Practice Lab: paper trading, simulator controls, backtests, strategy vault and paper journal.
- Settings: security and risk controls.

The Paper Trading Lab remains part of the application because it is useful for local practice, provider-integration testing and staged validation. It is intentionally not part of the main terminal.

The same repository can be cloned to a personal device for local use. Secrets and connected-account credentials must remain outside the repository.
